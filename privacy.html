Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Home Menu is a mobile app that helps a household and the person who cooks for it plan meals together. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Household information: household members and their roles, the kitchen stock you record, recipes you save, meal choices, and the dos and don'ts you set.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, show your household's kitchen stock and meal plans, and show each member the features their role allows.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, a database and authentication service. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Information is shared only within your household, as set by your household's admin. We do not share it with other third parties.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and your household's data by emailing YOUR_EMAIL.

Children

Home Menu is intended for adults who manage a household. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

المعلومات التي نجمعها

كيف نستخدمها

نستخدم هذه المعلومات فقط لتشغيل التطبيق: لتسجيل دخولك، وعرض مخزون مطبخ أسرتك وخطط الوجبات، وعرض الميزات التي يسمح بها دور كل عضو.

مكان التخزين

تُخزَّن معلوماتك لدى Supabase، وهي خدمة قواعد بيانات وتسجيل دخول. لا نبيع معلوماتك ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات.
